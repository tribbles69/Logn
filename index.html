Using a smartwatch
Turn on heart-rate broadcast on the watch first, then connect.
• Garmin: hold the menu button, then Settings, Sensors (or Wrist Heart Rate), Broadcast Heart Rate.
• Polar, Coros, Suunto, Amazfit: look for "HR broadcast" or "share heart rate" in the watch settings.
• Samsung Galaxy Watch, Wear OS: the watch connects but sends nothing on its own. Install a heart-rate broadcast app on the watch (search the Play Store on the watch for "heart rate broadcast" or "HR BLE"), start it, then connect here. If nothing arrives, also start a workout on the watch.
• Apple Watch and Fitbit can't send heart rate to a browser, so use a chest strap or arm band.
If the watch doesn't appear, try Not listed?, which shows every nearby device.
